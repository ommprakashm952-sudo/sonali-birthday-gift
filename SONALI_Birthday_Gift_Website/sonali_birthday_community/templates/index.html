A LETTER, WRITTEN WITH PURE LOVE

Dear Sonali,
our sister by heart.

Some relationships are not defined by family names or by how long we have known someone. They become special because of the place a person finds in our hearts. That is how we see you, Sonali — not just as a good friend, but as a wonderful sister by heart.

On your birthday, we wish you a life filled with happiness, courage, peaceful days, exciting dreams and people who always treat you with kindness and respect. May you keep learning, growing, laughing and becoming everything you dream of being. May every new chapter bring you beautiful opportunities and memories worth keeping forever.

This website is our little digital gift for you. It cannot be wrapped in paper, tied with a ribbon or placed in a box — but we hope every photo, every line and every little detail reminds you that you are valued, appreciated and remembered with affection.

Rishton ki khoobsurati naam se nahi, ehsaas se hoti hai,
Kuch khaas logon se hi zindagi mein mithaas hoti hai.
Dost bankar aaye, dil mein behen si jagah bana gaye,
Dua hai tumhari har subah khushi laaye.
Har sapna tumhara roshni ban kar khil jaaye,
Tumhari zindagi mein pyaar aur sukoon sada reh jaaye.

Happy Birthday, Sonali! Keep smiling, keep shining, and always remember: you have a special place in our hearts — like a sister, today and always. 💗
